# Data placeholders

This repository ships **analysis and processing code**. Large rasters and study-specific polygon references are not redistributed here.

## Public inputs (obtain from providers)

- **AlphaEarth Foundations annual embeddings** via Google Earth Engine (Brown et al. 2025).
- **Sentinel-1 / Sentinel-2** via Google Earth Engine or the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem.
- **SRTM / terrain** via Earth Engine (e.g. `USGS/SRTMGL1_003`).
- **Apple Orchard Maps of China (AOMC)** as cited in Xie et al. (2026), *Scientific Data*: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07197-0
- **ESA WorldCover** (diagnostics): Zanaga et al. (2022), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221

## Study-specific materials (on request)

Interpreted Gongliu and Xinyuan polygon references, visual-interpretation tables, field-supported attributes, trained model objects, and wall-to-wall prediction rasters are available from the corresponding author (Umut Hasan) on reasonable request, subject to base-imagery licensing and any map-approval constraints that apply to boundary overlays.
